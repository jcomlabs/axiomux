# Research Synthesis

## Procedure

1. Define the decision the research must support.
2. Prefer current primary sources and record source URL, license and retrieval date.
3. Extract mechanisms and evidence, not marketing adjectives.
4. Separate reusable ideas from code that cannot be incorporated under its license.
5. Note contradictions, confidence and gaps.
6. Convert accepted findings into explicit architecture decisions and tests.
